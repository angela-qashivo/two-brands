## 6. Qashivo — Credit Control for SMEs

Qashivo provides AI-powered credit-control technology to help SMEs manage outstanding invoices and customer communications.

Where a business uses Qashivo, personal information may be processed to:

- Identify outstanding invoices and overdue balances.
- Prioritise credit-control activity.
- Send payment reminders and follow-up communications.
- Contact customers through email, SMS and telephone.
- Capture responses and payment intentions.
- Record promises to pay and payment arrangements.
- Identify disputes and exceptions.
- Manage credit-control workflows and escalation processes.

The business using Qashivo will generally act as the **data controller** for its customer and debtor information.

## 7. Qonfirm — Independent Debt Verification

Qonfirm provides independent debt-verification technology for Invoice Finance providers.

It enables lenders to select clients, debtors and invoices for verification and obtain structured results.

Personal information may be processed to:

- Validate business and debtor contact information.
- Send independent verification requests.
- Confirm invoice amounts and outstanding balances.
- Record debtor responses.
- Follow up with non-responders.
- Identify discrepancies, disputes and verification exceptions.
- Maintain evidence and audit trails.
- Produce client-level and portfolio-level reports.

Verification requests may be issued through an independent third-party auditor acting within the agreed service arrangements.

Qonfirm may also process account and contact information relating to staff at borrower businesses invited onto the platform by an Invoice Finance provider.

Depending on the contractual and operational arrangements, the relevant Invoice Finance provider, borrower or another organisation may act as data controller.

Nexus KPI Limited generally acts as a processor where handling information on that controller's instructions.

## 8. If You Have Received a Communication from Qashivo or Qonfirm

You may receive an email, SMS message or telephone call through Qashivo or Qonfirm even if you have never visited our website or registered for our services.

This section explains why.

### Why Have I Been Contacted?

You may have been contacted because:

- A business you trade with uses Qashivo to manage its outstanding invoices and credit control; or
- An Invoice Finance provider uses Qonfirm to independently verify an invoice or outstanding balance.

### Where Did You Obtain My Information?

Your details may have been obtained from the accounting records, sales ledger, customer records or other authorised information sources used by the relevant business.

Information may include:

- Your name and company.
- Your business email address or telephone number.
- Invoice references and outstanding amounts.
- Payment and communication history.
- Information relevant to invoice verification.

### Who Controls My Information?

The business responsible for the invoice or the relevant Invoice Finance organisation will generally be the data controller, depending on the service and circumstances.

Qashivo normally acts as a processor carrying out instructions for that organisation.

The communication you receive should identify the relevant business. You may contact that organisation regarding the underlying invoice or your personal information.

You may also contact Qashivo at **hello@qashivo.com**, and we will help direct your enquiry appropriately.

### How Might You Contact Me?

Communications may take place through:

- Email.
- SMS.
- Telephone.
- Automated messaging systems.
- AI-powered voice systems.

Some communications may be drafted, scheduled or sent automatically.

Where telephone calls are conducted by an AI voice agent, the recipient should be informed that they are interacting with an automated system.

Calls may be recorded or transcribed where enabled and lawfully permitted, with appropriate notice provided.

### What Happens to My Response?

Your response may be recorded and shared with the relevant business or Invoice Finance provider.

For example, we may record that:

- An invoice has been paid.
- A payment date has been agreed.
- An amount is disputed.
- An invoice balance is confirmed.

- No response was received.

This information supports the organisation's credit-control, reconciliation or verification processes.

### Can I Ask You to Stop Contacting Me?

You may request that communications stop through a particular channel, or raise an objection to processing where applicable.

We will handle these requests in accordance with the relevant controller's instructions and applicable legal obligations.

Stopping contact through one channel does not necessarily remove an underlying payment obligation or prevent communications that are otherwise legally permitted.

### Can I Request Human Assistance?

Yes.

If you have concerns about an automated communication, you may request human assistance or ask for the matter to be reviewed by an appropriate person.

Contact **hello@qashivo.com** or the organisation identified in the communication.
