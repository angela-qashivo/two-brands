- Inter only (400/600/700/800); headlines 800 tight tracking; no italics; sentence case everywhere except eyebrows/wordmarks.
- Paper #FAF8F3 body sections, Night #0B1322 hero/header/closing bands/footer.
- Qashivo teal #0FB7C4 (ink #097A84), Qonfirm green #22DEAA (ink #0D7D5E); never both in one component. Qonfirm pages fully green.
- On Paper accent never fills buttons or text (use accent-ink, navy buttons); on Night one accent-filled primary CTA with navy text.
- Eyebrow: 11px uppercase 1.6px tracking ink-muted after 18x1.5px accent rule.
- CTAs: "Book a demo" (SMEs), "Discuss a pilot" (lenders), same words in header, hero, closing band.
- Writing: UK English, no em/en dashes, "cashflow" one word, lowercase "invoice finance", no exclamation marks/emoji; SME state words chasing/promised/paid/disputed/flagged; Qonfirm six categories exact wording.
- Hairlines not shadows; radii 6/8/12/pill; illustrative dashboards labelled "Illustrative data".
